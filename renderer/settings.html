The pill

Keep it on screen
Between dictations it rests as a small outline — no level, no wording — so you can see where it is and drag it without waiting for a recording. It opens to full size the moment you start talking.
Follow the mouse between monitors
The pill sits at the bottom of whichever screen your mouse is on. Drag the pill somewhere of your own while it is up and this switches itself off, and it stays where you put it. Switch it back on to return it to the bottom of the screen you are working on.
